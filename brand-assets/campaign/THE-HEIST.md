# THE HEIST — LUPIN. campaign book

**Steal the look. | اسرق الأنظار**

LUPIN. is the brand. Every drop is a mission, every piece has an operation name, and the "theft" is fiction: we steal the spotlight, never the stock. This book turns the brand brief into shots, copy and rules you can hand to a photographer, an AI image tool or a designer.

Pieces named here are the ones already in the store and in `../social/SOCIAL-KIT.md`. Add a new piece only once it is listed on lupinwear.store.

---

## 0. Rules before anything else

| Rule | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Real product, real photo | The garment in any ad is a **photo of the actual piece we sell**. AI or 3D may build the scene around it, never the garment. No retouching of prints, logos, colours, cut or labels beyond normal light/colour correction. |
| No invented Bershka items | Only pieces we have in stock. No "Bershka x LUPIN", no recolours, no mock-ups of items we can't sell. |
| No partnership implied | Bershka is named only as the maker of a piece ("Bershka graffiti hoodie"). Never Bershka logos as a campaign graphic, never their store, bags, staff uniforms or ad style. LUPIN. is the only brand mark on the frame. |
| Fiction is labelled | Any heist scene carries the small line: *Fictional campaign. All pieces are 100% original and legally sourced.* / *إعلان تمثيلي. كل القطع أصلية ١٠٠٪ ومن مصادر قانونية.* |
| Honest offer | Price, old price, sizes and stock match the product page on the day it is posted. "Limited" only when the quantity really is limited. |
| No real places | Store sets are generic or built ("a fashion vault"), never a recognisable real mall or a real Bershka store. |

Independent-reseller line for the bio / site footer:
> LUPIN. is an independent reseller. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Bershka or Inditex.
> لوبين متجر مستقل، ومش تابع لبرشكا أو إنديتكس.

## 1. Look and voice

- **Palette:** black `#0B0B0B`, dark brown `#2B1E16`, beige `#D8C8B0`, muted gold `#A88B4A`. Gold is for one detail per frame (a key, a tag, a line of type), not fills.
- **Light:** low-key, one hard key light, deep shadows, practical lights (desk lamp, monitor glow, exit sign). Warm tungsten vs. cool monitor blue only in the security room.
- **Lens:** 35 mm and 85 mm looks, shallow depth of field, slight film grain. No fisheye, no stickers, no cartoon, no sale-banner graphics.
- **The thief:** faceless or half-lit, black tailored layers, black leather gloves. He is the frame; the LUPIN. piece is the subject.
- **Voice:** short, calm, sure. One idea per line. No exclamation marks. No "🔥 SALE 🔥".

Type: the LUPIN. wordmark from `../LUPIN — Cap Logo@2x.png`, mission number in small caps with wide letter-spacing, nothing else on the image.

## 3. Mission 001 — The Bershka Heist: shot list

Each scene = a background plate (shoot it, or generate it with the prompt) + the real product photo composited in, or the real piece worn by a model on set. Formats: 4:5 Instagram feed, 1:1 Facebook, 9:16 Story/Reel.

### Scene 1 — The Blueprint (teaser, no product)
Overhead shot. Gloved hands spread a blueprint of a store floor plan on a dark wood desk; a brass key, a torn LUPIN. tag, a cold coffee. Plan marked "MISSION 001".
**Text on image:** `MISSION 001` / `Steal the look.`

### Scene 2 — The Security Room
A wall of CCTV monitors in a dark room; one screen frames the rail where the hero piece hangs. The thief's silhouette and gloved hand on the joystick in the foreground.
**Product:** real photo of **The Alibi** (Bershka oversized hoodie) placed inside the centre monitor as the "feed".

### Scene 3 — The Rail
Low angle down a long clothing rail in an empty, after-hours boutique. One hanger lit by a single spotlight. A gloved hand reaches in from frame edge.
**Product:** **The Brooklyn Job** (Bershka graffiti hoodie, cream) on the lit hanger — real photo.

### Scene 4 — The Getaway
Night, wet street, idling black car, door open. The thief walks away from camera toward it, so the key print on the back of the hoodie is the hero; a LUPIN. shopping bag on the back seat.
**Product:** **The Master Key** (Bershka graphic hoodie, stone) worn by a model (shoot) — no AI on the garment.

### Scene 5 — The Vault
A steel vault door half open; inside, folded pieces on lit shelves and a black LUPIN. bag with the gold sticker (`../packaging/`).
**Product:** the three Bershka hoodies (The Alibi, The Master Key, The Brooklyn Job) folded on the front shelf — real photos.

### Scene 6 — Mission Complete (the order)
A LUPIN. bag on a doorstep in soft dawn light, a handwritten card: "Mission complete."
Ties the fiction to the real thing: delivery across Egypt.

Reel order (15–20 s): 1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 4 → 6, 2–3 s each, end card `lupinwear.store`.

## 4. Background prompts (for AI image tools)

Use these for **plates only**, then composite the real product photo. Add to the end of every prompt:
`no text, no logos, no brand names, no clothing in focus, photorealistic, 4K, editorial fashion campaign, film grain`

1. **Blueprint:** `overhead shot of black leather-gloved hands spreading an architectural floor-plan blueprint on a dark walnut desk, brass key, coffee cup, single warm desk lamp, deep shadows, black dark brown beige and muted gold palette`
2. **Security room:** `dark security control room, wall of glowing CCTV monitors, one empty bright central screen, silhouette of a man in a black tailored coat from behind, leather glove on a joystick, cool blue monitor light vs warm tungsten practical, cinematic low-key lighting`
3. **Rail:** `after-hours minimalist luxury boutique, long brass clothing rail with empty hangers, one hanger under a single hard spotlight, polished dark stone floor, beige walls, black leather-gloved hand entering from the frame edge, cinematic 35mm low angle`
4. **Getaway (plate):** `night city street after rain, black sedan idling with rear door open, wet asphalt reflections, sodium streetlights, empty back seat, cinematic 35mm, moody`
5. **Vault:** `heavy steel bank-vault door half open, inside warmly lit wooden shelves with empty space at the front, dark brown and muted gold tones, cinematic symmetrical composition`
6. **Doorstep:** `apartment doorstep at dawn, soft warm light, beige wall, empty space for a shopping bag, calm, editorial still life`
